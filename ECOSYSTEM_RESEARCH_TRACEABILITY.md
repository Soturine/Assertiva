Use matched task/model/tools/env/budget, frozen independent oracle and hidden holdout, known-good/bad/ambiguous controls, grader mutation, metamorphic and counterfactual cases, repeated critical stochastic runs. Actual command/target/revision/version/consent/digests/exit/scope; source reading modality and limits. Observable traces and earliest supported critical failure, typed status/reason/retryability/next useful action; no private reasoning. Checkpoint/recovery and failure injection around effects; selective stale evidence only with verified impact.
